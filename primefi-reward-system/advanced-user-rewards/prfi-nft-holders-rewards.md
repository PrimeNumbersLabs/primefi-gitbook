# PRFI NFT Holders Rewards

The PRFI NFT collection is a Prime Numbers Labs collection with a live PRFI staking flow.

Qualifying participants can stake PRFI through a PRFI NFT without also opening a PrimeFi lending position or liquidity pair. This staking utility is separate from the planned use of NFTs as collateral in PrimeFi lending markets; NFT-as-lending-collateral is not part of the live PRFI NFT staking flow.

### Current staking rewards vs planned revenue share

PRFI NFT staking can participate in currently configured staking rewards. A separate future concept has been described as allocating 40% of PrimeFi lending-and-borrowing protocol profits to qualifying PRFI NFT participants.

The revenue-share component is **planned and should not be treated as active** until PrimeFi publishes an activation notice, a precise definition of profits and exclusions, the distribution contract, eligibility rules, and verifiable on-chain configuration. Ownership or staking does not currently guarantee that distribution, a payout, APR, or yield.

### **Time Frame**

The published program uses monthly reward periods. Where supported by the current app and contracts, a qualifying participant can choose to:

1. Manually claim the rewards.
2. Auto-compound them by restarting the lock-up period.

### **Meeting Conditions**

Current staking rewards, if any, vary with available reward funding, participant weights, and active configuration. Qualifying requires the participant to:

1. Own a PRFI NFT.
2. Deposit and lock the NFT through the live staking flow for the configured period.

The program uses a lock period to discourage participants from entering immediately before a distribution and exiting immediately afterward. The published minimum is 31 days, but users should verify the active lock options, eligibility status, and claimable amount in the app and on-chain before acting.

{% hint style="success" %}
These conditions are prerequisites for participation, but meeting them does not guarantee a distribution.
{% endhint %}
